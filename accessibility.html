Accessibility Statement

Updated: October 2026.

General

I believe every person has the right to use the web with dignity, equality, comfort and independence, and I aim for this site to be usable by everyone, including people with disabilities. The site aims to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG 2.1), level AA.

What this site does

  • Semantic HTML with a clear heading structure and a "skip to content" link.
  • Everything can be reached and used with a keyboard, with visible focus indicators.
  • Text and background colors are chosen for readable contrast.
  • Hebrew content is marked as right-to-left so screen readers and browsers handle it correctly.
  • Animations are turned off for visitors who set "reduce motion" in their system.
  • The layout adapts to any screen size and supports browser zoom.

Known limitations

Some content is embedded from or linked to third-party services (such as YouTube, Spotify, Medium and Substack). Their accessibility is outside my control.

Contact

If you have trouble with any content on this site, or want to report an accessibility issue, please reach out on LinkedIn and I'll be happy to help.
